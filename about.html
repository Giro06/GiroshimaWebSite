Giroshima is a one-person studio. I take mechanics players already love, add one twist, and get the result onto Steam in weeks, not years. No team, no office. Just me and the AI agents I direct.

What I'm after

Most small games die in development, and solo projects die fastest. Mine get finished. I keep scope tight, test ideas before committing to them, and put a playable build in front of players on day one.

  • Proven mechanic, one twist. Every idea starts as "known game + one change" and has to pass a scored concept gate before anyone builds it.
  • Built for PC players. No energy bars, no daily-login chores, no ads in my PC games. Pay once, play.
  • Fair price, global audience. Low prices, and every Steam release ships in 10 languages.
  • Fun you can see. Loud, readable, showy games that make sense in a five-second clip.
  • Marketing from data. AI-driven competitor analysis tells me what the best games in a genre show, say and charge. My store pages, capsules and trailers are built on it.

How I work

A human-directed AI production line

I direct a team of AI agents across research, design, code, art, QA, localization and marketing. I make the calls and do the playtesting; agents do the volume. That is how one person ships like a small studio. The same playbook runs every game, and it gets sharper with each release.

🧑‍💻
  1. 01

    Market research

    AI agents analyze comparable Steam hits: tags, prices, capsules, reviews and wishlist signals. No idea starts without at least three proven games behind it.

  2. 02

    Concept gate

    5–10 pitches scored on 15 criteria. The best two or three get a mock capsule and a two-hour grey-box test.

  3. 03

    Playable

    A lightweight HTML/JS prototype, playable in days. Agents run automated QA on every build.

  4. 04

    Art & story

    Characters, cutscenes and UI art produced and iterated in parallel with gameplay.

  5. 05

    Playtest & polish

    Blunt human playtest notes turned into fix lists and resolved in parallel.

  6. 06

    10 languages

    Game text, store page and trailer captions localized before launch.

  7. 07

    AI-driven marketing

    Competitor analysis shows what the top games in the tag put on their capsules, trailers and store pages. Agents draft variants on that basis, I test and pick. Then outreach to creators and press.

  8. 08

    Ship on Steam

    Desktop build with Steamworks, review, release. Then the next game starts down the same road.
